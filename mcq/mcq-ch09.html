A $\dfrac{3t^2}{2}$

The polar point $(3, \pi/2)$ in Cartesian coordinates is
A $(3, 0)$
B $(0, -3)$
C $(0, 3)$
D $(3, 3)$
$x=r\cos\theta=3\cos(\pi/2)=0$; $y=r\sin\theta=3\sin(\pi/2)=3$. So $(0,3)$.

The area enclosed by the polar curve $r=2$ is
A $4\pi$
B $2\pi$
C $8\pi$
D $\pi$
$A=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{2\pi}r^2\,d\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{2\pi}4\,d\theta=4\pi$. (Circle of radius 2: $\pi r^2=4\pi$. ✓)

For the parametric curve $x=\cos t$, $y=\sin t$, the curve traces
A a parabola
B a line
C an ellipse
D a circle
$x^2+y^2=\cos^2t+\sin^2t=1$: the unit circle.
5
The arc length of the parametric curve $x=3t$, $y=4t$ on $0\le t\le1$ is
A 7
B $3+4$
C 5
D 12
$L=\int_0^1\sqrt{(dx/dt)^2+(dy/dt)^2}\,dt=\int_0^1\sqrt{9+16}\,dt=5\cdot1=5$.

The polar curve $r=1+\cos\theta$ is called a
A lemniscate
B cardioid
C rose curve
D limaçon with inner loop
$r=1+\cos\theta$ is a cardioid (heart-shaped), since the additive constant equals the coefficient of the trig term.

A $\langle 2t, e^t\rangle$

D $\langle t^3/3, e^t\rangle$
$\mathbf{v}(t)=\mathbf{r}'(t)=\langle 2t, e^t\rangle$.
8
The speed of $\mathbf{r}(t)=\langle 3\cos t,\, 3\sin t\rangle$ is
A 0
B $3$
C $\sqrt{9\cos^2t+9\sin^2t}$
D $3$
$\mathbf{v}=\langle-3\sin t, 3\cos t\rangle$. Speed$=|\mathbf{v}|=\sqrt{9\sin^2t+9\cos^2t}=3$. (Both C and D equal 3; D is the simplified numerical answer.)
9
Area enclosed by one petal of $r=\sin(2\theta)$ (a 4-petal rose) is
A $\pi/2$
B $\pi/4$
C $\pi/8$
D $\pi$
First petal: $0\le\theta\le\pi/2$. $A=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^2(2\theta)d\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\pi/2}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{8}$.
10
A parametric curve has a horizontal tangent when
A $dx/dt = 0$
B $dy/dt = 0$ (provided $dx/dt\ne 0$)
C $dy/dx = 1$
D $d^2y/dx^2 = 0$
Horizontal tangent: $dy/dx=0\Leftrightarrow dy/dt=0$ (and $dx/dt\ne0$). Vertical tangent: $dx/dt=0$ (and $dy/dt\ne0$).
11
Converting $r=3\sin\theta$ to rectangular form gives
A $x^2+(y-3/2)^2=9/4$
B $x^2+y^2=3$
C $y=3x$
D $(x-3)^2+y^2=9$
$r=3\sin\theta\Rightarrow r^2=3r\sin\theta\Rightarrow x^2+y^2=3y\Rightarrow x^2+y^2-3y=0\Rightarrow x^2+(y-3/2)^2=9/4$.

A 7
B 14
C 10
D $5\sqrt{2}$

A $4\pi$
B $8\pi$
C $10\pi$

$A=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{2\pi}(9-1)d\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}(8)(2\pi)=8\pi$.

The slope $dy/dx$ of the polar curve $r=f(\theta)$ is
A $\dfrac{f'(\theta)\sin\theta+f(\theta)\cos\theta}{f'(\theta)\cos\theta-f(\theta)\sin\theta}$

18
The parametric arc length formula is
A $\displaystyle\int_a^b\sqrt{x+y}\,dt$
B $\displaystyle\int_a^b\sqrt{\left(\tfrac{dx}{dt}\right)^2+\left(\tfrac{dy}{dt}\right)^2}\,dt$
C $\displaystyle\int_a^b\left(\tfrac{dy}{dt}/\tfrac{dx}{dt}\right)\,dt$
D $\displaystyle\int_a^b x'(t)y'(t)\,dt$
Arc length: $L=\int_a^b\sqrt{(x')^2+(y')^2}\,dt$, the integral of the speed.
19
The area enclosed by the cardioid $r=1+\sin\theta$ is
A $3\pi/2$
B $2\pi$
C $\pi$
D $3\pi$
$A=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{2\pi}(1+\sin\theta)^2d\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{2\pi}(1+2\sin\theta+\sin^2\theta)d\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}(2\pi+0+\pi)=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$.

The displacement of a particle from $t=0$ to $t=3$ with $\mathbf{v}(t)=\langle t, t^2\rangle$ is
A $\langle 3, 9\rangle$
B $\langle 3, 27\rangle$
C $\langle 4.5, 8\rangle$
D $\langle 9/2, 9\rangle$
$\displaystyle\int_0^3\langle t, t^2\rangle dt=\left\langle\dfrac{t^2}{2}, \dfrac{t^3}{3}\right\rangle_0^3=\left\langle\dfrac{9}{2}, 9\right\rangle$.
