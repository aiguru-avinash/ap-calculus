What's Inside

This course follows the College Board AP Calculus curriculum and Georgia state standards. It progresses from foundational limit theory through integration, differential equations, and into BC-only topics: parametric equations, polar coordinates, vectors, and infinite series.

📘 Calculus AB Topics
Limits, derivatives, integration, differential equations, and their real-world applications. Covers all 8 AB units.
📗 Calculus BC Topics
All AB content plus parametric & polar functions, vector calculus, and infinite sequences and series (Taylor, Maclaurin).

How to Use This Course

📌 Suggested Study Path
  1. Read each chapter section and work through the worked examples — try them yourself before revealing the solution.
  2. After each chapter, take the 20-question MCQ for that chapter. Aim for 80% before moving on.
  3. After completing all AB chapters (1–8), work through the 25 AB FRQs under timed conditions (15 min per question).
  4. BC students: continue to Chapters 9 & 10, then complete the 25 BC FRQs.
  5. Review mistakes using the detailed solutions and rubrics. Identify your weak chapters and re-read those sections.
