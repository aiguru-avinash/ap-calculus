Which Riemann sum uses the left endpoint of each subinterval?
A Left Riemann Sum
B Right Riemann Sum
C Midpoint Sum
D Trapezoidal Rule
The Left Riemann Sum evaluates $f$ at the left endpoint $x_{i-1}$ of each subinterval. The Right sum uses $x_i$; the Midpoint uses $\overline{x}_i=(x_{i-1}+x_i)/2$.
